Convert Typst to Word

Turn a .typ file or a ZIP project into a Microsoft Word document. The converter runs locally in your browser and can write equations as editable Word math.

Convert a file

  1. Open the converter and drag your file onto the page. To paste a snippet instead, select 粘贴代码 (Paste code).
  2. Select 转换为 .docx (Convert), then 下载 .docx (Download).
  3. Open the document in Word. Click inside a converted fraction to edit its numerator or denominator.

Try an editable fraction

The sample combines two groups of readings into a weighted mean. Convert its source yourself, or open the DOCX and click the fraction. The PDF shows how Typst typeset the same source.

Convert from the command line

Download the CLI for your platform, then run typst2word weighted-mean.typ weighted-mean.docx.
